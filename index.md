# The Climate Laboratory for ATMOS 6030

***A hands-on approach to climate physics and climate modeling***

**Adapted for ATMOS 6030 at the University of Utah by Margaret Duffy**

**Based on the [Climate Laboratory book][book] by [Brian E. J. Rose][brian], University at Albany**

This book is powered by [MyST Markdown][mystmd] and [JupyterBook 2][jupyterbook2]/

The material is mostly based on lecture notes for courses developed 
at the [University at Albany][ualbany].

Much of the content is made possible by [climlab][climlab], an open-source
Python toolkit for interactive, process-oriented climate modeling.
[Brian Rose][brian] is the principle developer of climlab as well as the author of the [Climate Laboratory book][book].
